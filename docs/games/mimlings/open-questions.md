# Mimlings — Open Questions

## Decided

- **(2026-09-25) v1 is a sandbox in short sessions.** It's a toy for playing
  with the idea: no server persistence, no goals, no leaderboard. Questions 1
  and 3 below are settled for v1.

## Open

1. **Session or persistent herd?** A 5–10 minute session with a verdict at the
   end is simple. A herd that lives on the server and keeps growing while you're
   away is truer to Thronglets/Tamagotchi, but a much bigger build.
2. **How dark can it go?** Can flinging kill, or only dizzy/scare? Should a
   mean herd ever hurt each other visibly, or just become anxious and scattered?
3. **Is there a goal?** Sandbox first, then teaching challenges? Or a scored
   "harmony" leaderboard?
4. **Populous terrain?** Raise/lower land, ponds, rivers would give cultures
   natural borders. Worth it in v1, or a later layer?
5. **What does the verdict say, and when?** End of session, on demand, or
   when the population hits milestones?
6. **Which animal?** One species (round hamster/chick/blob-bunny), or several
   that learn differently?
7. **Sight model**: just a radius, or facing direction too (you can sneak up
   behind them and not be seen)?
8. **Does the hand ever run out?** A limited number of berries or gestures per
   day would make each demonstration count.
