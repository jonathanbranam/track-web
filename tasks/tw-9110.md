The ticket holds the human's words, the research and the v1 design. Build **Update 4** of `docs/tickets/open/games-hex-block-puzzle-hex-frvr-style-drag-hex-pieces-up-ont-46jx.md`:
- hexagon board, R = 4 (61 cells)
- pieces of 1–4 hexes, mixed red/blue/green, with bird/note/leaf outline symbols
- 3-piece tray, no rotation
- full lines along the 3 axes clear
- scoring includes a ×3 bonus for a one-colour line, and symbol counters
