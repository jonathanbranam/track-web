## Ideas so far

- **"Flow games"** (an arrow tap-away puzzle). The human clarified on 2026-10-04 (verbatim):

  > Flow games have arrows that curve around and point in different directions and you tap the one that points to the exit and it "flows" away. The puzzle is to do them in order

  So it's not flow Inc. and not Flow Free. It's the tap-away arrow genre: a board of curving arrows, each pointing a different way. Tapping an arrow whose path to the exit is clear makes it flow off the board. Arrows that are blocked have to wait, so the puzzle is finding the order that clears the board. Not asked to be built yet.
- **Under the Hole** (working name; the opposite of Hole.io). The human, 2026-10-05, verbatim:

  > This would be another casual game idea, and I'm not really sure how the gameplay goes. The idea is that it's the opposite of Hole.io. By opposite, I mean think about what's happening in Hole.io: you are a hole that's eating everything around you. Imagine: where is this stuff going? What's happening on the other side of this hole?
  >
  > I'm imagining somebody who's sitting underneath the level. They're kind of a janitor, a maintenance person, or somebody trying to keep the place clean. They've just finished cleaning up, maybe whatever it is down there. I don't know what's down there, but they've finished tidying everything up, and then they look up. Way up in the sky, five holes open up, and they think, "Oh no, not again." As soon as the holes open up, they start racing around in the sky, dropping stuff everywhere.
  >
  > That's just kind of the rough idea. This person would be, again, I'm not sure, cleaning it up. I don't think it would be another hole that seems weird. I was thinking of someone pushing a broom around, trying to clean everything up really fast, or burning everything. I don't have any solid ideas yet, but kind of like the inverse of this: what's happening on the other side of that hole
