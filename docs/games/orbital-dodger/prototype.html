Physics

Gravity is inverse-square: F = G · (r² × mass scale) / d². Bigger planets already pull harder via r² — mass scale multiplies that further. Changes apply live, mid-run.

Fuel

Holding to thrust drains fuel over time. Reaching zero ends the round, even mid-orbit — so it's spent, not just crashed.

Scoring

Points accrue slowly out in open space and ramp up the closer you fly to a planet's surface (up to the proximity range below), rewarding close orbits over just parking far away.

Forward path guide

Faint line projects where gravity alone (ignoring your thrust) would carry the ship, so you can read an upcoming orbit or collision.

Orbital Dodger

Hold anywhere to thrust toward your finger — thrusting drains your fuel, so use it wisely. Ride the gravity, don't touch a planet, grab the gold stars.
